# Group reward stand

Lobby kiosk for the group gift (decision 114, `docs/design/GROUP_REWARD.md`): a dark Stained Glass plinth, a mini
Marble Kiln, a round aqua window with three gold friends (the group emblem, original), glass marbles, and a floating
glass heart (`GlassHeart`, origin at its centre; the client bobs it).

- Build: `tools/blender/build_group_reward_stand.py` (scene `GroupRewardStand`), 1,536 tris in 12 meshes.
- Files: `GroupRewardStand.fbx`, `group_reward_stand.blend`, `preview.png`.
- Upload: Open Cloud, group 11116787, Model **91987420283885** (`roblox_assets.json`, list `upload_list.json`).
- In Studio the importer comes in at 100x: divide each MeshPart's Size and Position by 100. Colours and materials are
  set in Studio (listed in the design doc).
