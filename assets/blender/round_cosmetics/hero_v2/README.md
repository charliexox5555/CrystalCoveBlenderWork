# Blender round heroes v2

Creator-directed replacement for the native-part prototypes, October 16, 2026. Original IDs/unlocks and previous assets preserved. Source: `round_heroes.blend`; builder: `tools/blender/build_round_hero_art.py`. Original Blender scene remains open and unchanged.

## Import needed

Import `ROUND_HEROES_V2_IMPORT.fbx` with Studio's **3D Importer**, scale **1**, preserving the **RH2_** mesh names. Leave the imported model in Workspace. Do not manually resize/anchor/recolor it: the installer handles that after all 32 meshes upload successfully. Do not publish the place.

Codex then loads `tools/studio/round_hero_import_data.json` into `_G.RoundHeroImportData` and executes `tools/studio/install_round_hero_imports.luau` through Studio MCP in Edit. Preflight rejects missing/duplicate/unuploaded meshes before changing live art. Originals back up to `ServerStorage.RoundHeroesBeforeV2`; imported originals remain in Workspace. Four ride models replace live sources; five VFX meshes go to `ReplicatedStorage.Assets.VFX.RoundHeroV2`, with matching finisher portraits. Runtime selects these automatically. Lobby display clones still need refreshing after import.

**Status: exported/verified, not installed.** Automatic upload returned “CreateAssetAsync and CreateAssetVersionAsync are not available yet.” Play-mode EditableMesh requires an experience setting that was left unchanged. No security/device changes, paid activation or publication. Temporary Edit-mode mesh copies verified effect branches and were removed; they are not permanent assets.

## Art

- Rocket Skiff: curved hull/recessed deck, paired nacelles, swept fins, nozzles and animated exhaust.
- Skyboard: lifted surfboard silhouette, curved rails, inlay and lift glow.
- Cloud Cushion: unified scalloped cloud, cushion and flowing rainbow ribbons.
- Prism Ray: organic body, tapered swept wings, wing-following inlays, eyes and curved tail; separate hinge metadata.
- VFX: jagged ShockCrown, lobed BlastBloom, beveled NovaStar, faceted CrystalShard and curled ConfettiCurl. Bubble spheres remain native; original texture particles add glints/pressure.

1 Blender unit = 1 stud. Deck is z=0, +Y forward; Roblox coordinates (x,z,-y). VFX face Roblox XY. Each mesh is below 2,000 triangles; all 32 total **11,974**. FBX round-trip: matching topology, maximum bounds error **0.00000535 studs**, no negative volumes. Colors/materials are assigned in Studio; pale render materials are previews. `manifest.json` lists authoritative meshes. Earlier `prism_ray_WingInlay.json` and `rocket_preview.png` drafts are retained but not used by the manifest/bundle.
