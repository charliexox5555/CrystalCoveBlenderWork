# Glass Vs Glass marketing direction

Updated 23 October 2026 after the creator's rename and Plays-ad results. Historical asset/source names remain Glass Clash.

- **Current goal: an immediately understandable activity and reason to play.** The creator renamed the game Glass Vs Glass and requested a different concept family after the new/previous Plays creatives clustered around 1% CTR. Better-looking effects or another creature swap are insufficient as the next experiment.
- Simple should still feel exciting. The creator rejected calm product portraits as boring; that preference was not a measured campaign result. Keep vivid color, a clear action, choice or rivalry. Expressive faces and powers are optional tools, not requirements or demonstrated causes of high CTR.
- The previous shared composition became elaborate: shiny fantasy creatures, arena beams, reaction avatars, ropes, shards, motion and HUD. A clarity problem is a hypothesis. Test recognizable glass breaking, the real three-offer fighter selection, and a bold two-object matchup as distinct promises. Decorative coins/confetti or obscuring white bursts are not substitutes for an event.
- Use original Glass Clash glassies and actual suspension/aiming/collecting mechanics. Competitor images are visual storytelling references, not assets to copy or proof of CTR performance.
- Saved bacon boy/girl identities should look like real Roblox avatars. Prefer classic block-ended R6 arms when hand geometry is unreliable. Avoid human fingers, invented fists and malformed C grips; check shoulders, wrists and pose direction.
- Asset shape and color should remain recognizable. Expressive marketing faces can be exaggerated when following the current brief; do not present illustrations as untouched screenshots or direct renders.
- Latest Plays snapshot: Bottle Breaks Dragon 36 clicks/3,493 impressions (1.03% calculated); Bear Panic 29/3,366 (0.86%); Power Duel 113/9,859 (1.15%); Last Heart 48/4,921 (0.98%). Total 226/21,639 = 1.04%. No convincing separation; Power Duel is only the highest observed baseline. Reporting dates and plays/CPP are not visible.
- Historical Engagement snapshot favored Power Duel 2.4%/327 and Bear Panic 2.1%/283, about eight/six clicks. Those early leads were provisional and did not establish the new Plays performance. Unicorn's earlier 2.3%/663 became 1.4%/2,096. Do not blend objectives/cohorts/time windows or attribute the difference to the rename alone.
- Text/logo treatment is a test variable, not a proven universal rule. This reset uses no text, PICK ONE and VS for different concepts. The older provisional Unicorn leader contained a logo. None of these comparisons isolates the effect of typography.
- Keep ad CTR/plays and organic Home qPTR separate. Save creator feedback and comparable asset-level results with each test set.
- The creator has now tested Plays. Keep objective/name/settings steady for the next concept comparison, retain one current baseline and compare actual plays/cost per play as well as CTR. Do not keep prescribing the objective switch as though it has not occurred. A 4–5% CTR is the creator's goal, not a guaranteed result or universal benchmark.
