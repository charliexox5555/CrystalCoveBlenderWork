# Glass Clash marketing preferences

Updated 23 October 2026 from direct creator feedback and the latest Engagement-ad screenshot.

- **Simple composition should still feel exciting.** The creator rejected calm one-glassy product portraits on plain gradients as boring. They want expressive subjects, vivid power color, motion and a clear event, closer to the supplied Blade Ball composition references.
- Use a dominant subject and readable action/reaction grouping. Flash can come from a panicking face, dramatic perspective, glow tied to a particular power and an approaching opponent. Decorative coins/confetti or a large obscuring white burst are not substitutes for a story.
- Use original Glass Clash glassies and actual suspension/aiming/collecting mechanics. Competitor images are visual storytelling references, not assets to copy or proof of CTR performance.
- Saved bacon boy/girl identities should look like real Roblox avatars. Prefer classic block-ended R6 arms when hand geometry is unreliable. Avoid human fingers, invented fists and malformed C grips; check shoulders, wrists and pose direction.
- Asset shape and color should remain recognizable. Expressive marketing faces can be exaggerated when following the current brief; do not present illustrations as untouched screenshots or direct renders.
- The latest Engagement-ad snapshot favors Power Duel at 2.4% CTR/327 impressions (about eight clicks) and Bear Panic at 2.1%/283 (about six). These are provisional controls with small samples, not proof that a logo, avatar, palette, expression or specific creature caused performance. Unicorn shatter is now 1.4%/2,096, superseding its earlier 2.3%/663 result.
- Logo-free art is a current design choice, not a proven universal rule. The earlier provisional Unicorn leader contained a logo and used a purple background.
- Keep ad CTR/plays and organic Home qPTR separate. Save creator feedback and comparable asset-level results with each test set.
- The creator's current priority is cheaper player acquisition. Current Roblox guidance says Engagement targets a smaller highly engaged audience and typically has higher cost/play. Test a separate Plays campaign with the existing leading art first, then add one creative challenger at a time; compare actual plays/cost per play as well as CTR. A 4–5% CTR is the creator's goal, not a guaranteed result or universal benchmark. See the cited objective guidance in the latest test plan.
