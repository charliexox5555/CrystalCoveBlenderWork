## Research and design choices

[Roblox's official icon guidance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/experience-icons), checked 22 October 2026, specifies square art at least 512 × 512, recommends checking clarity at 150 × 150, and stresses relevant imagery, color and contrast. It also states that each locale displays one icon. The no-logo direction comes from the creator; the documentation does not establish that removing a logo increases conversion.

Applied here: large recognizable glassies, orange/cyan separation for the duel, green/indigo separation for the Dragon, visible suspension strings and breakage, no wordmark, and fewer peripheral effects. The duel's two faces were enlarged after the first small-size check. The official page's quality/color examples were also consulted. An attempted public Ball VS Ball image fetch failed, so no visual conclusions were drawn from that icon.

Recommendation is a visual/gameplay hypothesis, not measured Glass Clash performance. Previous Cookie Inc qPTR results do not establish which Glass Clash icon will win. Keep ads and Home results separate; a sequential icon swap alone cannot isolate audience/time changes.
