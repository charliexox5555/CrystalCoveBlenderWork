# Uploading the Glass Clash thumbnails

All files are in `final/`: 1920×1080 JPG, each under 3 MB (about 0.5 MB), logo included. `final/clean/` has the
same images without the logo, plus 4 spares. `final/contact_sheet.png` shows them at phone size (300 px).
Rebuild with `python3 tools/marketing/compose.py` (needs Pillow).

## Steps

1. Go to **Creator Dashboard** → **Creations** → **Glass Clash** → **Configure** (or **Places** → the start place → **Thumbnails**).
2. Open the **Home Page** thumbnails tab.
3. Click **Edit active thumbnails** → **Upload**. Upload the 10 files above, in order.
4. Tick **Active** on all 10 (personalization needs **2 or more** active), then **Save**.
5. Start the **personalization test** if the page asks. Roblox first shows each thumbnail to random players, then favours the best one for each group of players.

## After one week

1. On the thumbnails page, look at the **qualified play-through rate** for each thumbnail.
2. Replace the bottom 2–3 with spares or new variants of the winning **type** (for example, if Action wins, make more Action shots with other glassies).
3. If the logo spot matters (top-left vs top-centre), you'll see it in T3a vs T3b and T1a vs T1b.
4. After Halloween, replace `T1h` with a spare.
5. Keep at most 10 active. Change only a few at a time so the results stay readable.
