## What can improve next — art assessment, not conversion proof
1. T3a is the strongest current read: closest glassy clash with readable amber/cyan silhouettes and clear opposing reactions. Start with it as a visual hypothesis, not a proven qPTR leader.
2. T3b uses considerable space for logo and stand; next composition variant could reduce logo footprint and enlarge the clash while keeping the suspension hooks understandable. Character replacement did not test this.
3. Original impact flash overexposes the glass contact. A modest local reduction of bloom/rays could reveal cracks and edges better without removing impact.
4. T5a has King, crown, logo, glassies, crowd and distant islands all competing. Next variant could subordinate crowd/islands and show a larger clash; keep genuine crown status and actual game setting.
5. Preserve the matte world / glossy-glassy contrast. Avatar skin/clothes should not become as shiny as collectibles.
6. The marked emotional faces are useful, but keep reactions aimed into the fight rather than generic front-facing portraits.
