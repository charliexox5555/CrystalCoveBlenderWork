# Glass Clash marketing image report

Completed 14 thumbnail sections with two options each: 28 PNG files, all verified at1920×1080. I1, I2 and I3 are deferred at the creator’s request; no icon was selected or exported. Best-icon ranking is therefore deferred.

Created with the built-in image_gen tool. Every listed available beauty, subject, background and Studio reference was opened; shared references were reused after inspection. Ball VS Ball reference PNGs were absent; references/README.md was read and its energy/composition guidance used without copying content. The tool accepts at most five reference files per generation, so T3/T5 used the beauty, subject and the most relevant Studio/scene inputs after all listed files had been viewed.

Each option was visually compared with its source and checked for invented toys, text/UI/coins, facial/shape/color changes, clear zones, bottom-overlay conflicts, game-world style and brand resemblance. Failed attempts were regenerated, with at most three generation attempts per option. The selected and alternate candidates were also reviewed at300px wide in .qa/mobile_review_1.png and .qa/mobile_review_2.png. Labels in those QA sheets are outside the marketing images. Final files contain no observed text, logos, UI, coins or watermark.

Strict exact-preservation is not fully achieved: generative relighting changes refraction/highlights, and some small facial details, toy proportions and crown/crowd details drift from the source. These are the best retained candidates, not a claim that every candidate passes every rejection rule. Specific layout and identity limits are listed below. The collection source renders already crop the common row; those partial bodies remain occluded. No claim is made that unseen source geometry was recovered.

Tool-native output dimensions varied; the chosen outputs were copied and resampled with macOS sips to the required exact size. No creative retouching was performed outside image_gen. No source render, code, roadmap, devlog or other file outside from_chatgpt/ was edited; repository pull/commit/push and code tests were not run because this task was limited to marketing output files. Existing unrelated changes were left alone.

Top10 thumbnail ranking (visual/editorial judgment, not measured click-through performance):

1. **T1c** — Strongest jewel contrast; orange Phoenix and pale-blue Comet separate clearly from the dark sky.
2. **T1a** — Bright clash, clear faces and a useful calm top-left area; the clearest golden-hour action candidate.
3. **T1h** — Pumpkin silhouette, mint face and violet Orb make the seasonal theme immediate without text.
4. **T2a** — One large, friendly Dragon face and a broad clear zone; shatter direction remains clear on a phone.
5. **T3a** — Large opposing reactions frame a readable Bear/Blade clash and explain the player-versus-player moment.
6. **T2c** — Pink Unicorn and directional shards stand out cleanly against the dark blue sky.
7. **T5a** — King, five-tip crown and nearby clash communicate Crown Hill with a clear foreground focal point.
8. **T3b** — Balanced avatar reactions and centred gameplay work well, although the glassies are smaller than T3a.
9. **T1d** — A useful centred night variant with readable opposing colors and more headroom than T1c.
10. **T4a** — Adds collection variety and rarity progression to the upload set; detailed toy identities read less well at 300 px.

The remaining thumbnails rank lower because T1b and T2b sacrifice focal size for headroom, T4b has tight clear-zone space and dense small objects, and T5b makes the central clash small. Best icon: deferred.
