## What this changes

- The Unicorn is the observed CTR leader and has the most impressions. Preserve the exact currently serving Unicorn image as the control. Do not replace it merely because the creator initially described the overall campaign as doing poorly.
- The two-player composition is the observed laggard, but its 0.5% is approximately one click. One additional click would put it near 1.0%. The ordering is provisional.
- Design hypothesis worth testing: a single distinctive glassy, large in frame, with clear breakage and contrast may attract clicks better than the tested broader avatar/collection compositions. These creatives change several things at once, so the screenshot cannot identify which design feature caused performance.
- The leading Unicorn still has its logo and uses a purple scene. The evidence does not establish that deleting logos, switching to daylight or removing avatars will improve CTR.
- The provided competitor examples have no performance data. They remain composition references, not demonstrated winning benchmarks.
