## What changed after feedback

The first attempt equated simplicity with a nearly empty gradient and calmly smiling collectibles. The creator rejected that. The useful reference principles are a large readable expression, an obvious threat/move, extreme foreground/background scale contrast, vivid opposing colors and motion with a visible source.

Bear Panic uses a comic worried expression, body recoil, a close low camera, cyan power arcs around the incoming blade and a real arena floor to create an event. Power Duel follows the third reference's clean action/reaction grouping and outlined direction cue, using real Glass Clash subjects and the actual suspended arena.

The original model identities remain recognizable. Facial expressions are intentionally exaggerated for key art, following the creator's correction; the artwork does not establish that native collectibles currently animate those expressions. The enemy remains a suspended glassy rather than a player-held sword. The aiming arrow/cursor is explanatory artwork, not asserted live UI.
