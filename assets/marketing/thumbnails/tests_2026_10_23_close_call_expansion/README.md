## Game fidelity

Original Pop/Jam/Prism Blade/Frostglobe/Hot Sauce/Honey Bear identities are retained, with exaggerated marketing expressions. These are illustrations, not untouched screenshots or direct Blender renders.

Frost is grounded in the Frostglobe kit. Bomb, Patch and Bumpers are grounded in current ArenaRules/ArenaFX: dark sphere with orange fuse nub, green glass orb/core with heart marker, cyan glass spheres on rods. Pair those three new concepts with the matching published round revamp. No instant-kill, currency, free-item, permanent-freeze or stronger-paid-item promise is depicted.
