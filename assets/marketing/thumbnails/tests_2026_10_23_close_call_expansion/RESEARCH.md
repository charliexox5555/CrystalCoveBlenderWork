# Research and creative rationale — 23 October 2026

## Our strongest evidence

The creator's first comparable crop gives Close Call 18 clicks/644 impressions (2.80%), Lid Block 9/648 (1.39%) and Cast Contrast 5/603 (0.83%). Each showed 0.04 ad credits and Learning. Close Call is an early lead, not a confirmed stable 2.80% baseline. The creator prefers this direction and now wants new concepts as well as improvements, aiming for **5–8% ad CTR**.

Large vulnerability, visible damage and an approaching threat are a useful combined hypothesis. This evidence does not isolate facial expression, no text, blue background, opponent identity or a specific rendering treatment. It supplies no attributed plays or retention for these three designs. Original Close Call is retained unchanged and its hash matches the prior pack.

## Primary sources reviewed

1. **Roblox thumbnail documentation:** recommends relevant, unambiguous game imagery, theme-appropriate colors, 16:9 presentation and keeping essential material away from the bottom metadata area. We apply those to actual glass battles and clear interaction. The page's personalization results concern Home qualified play-through rate, not paid-ad CTR; they are not a target forecast. [Official documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails)
2. **Roblox's Hypershot case:** the team expanded from 10 to 25 unique creatives to identify effective concepts. The case gives no per-image CTR or universal winning formula. The useful lesson is broader concept exploration plus measurement, which is why this set contains danger, rivalry, a resource contest and a physics surprise. [Official case](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise)
3. **Ads Manager definitions:** paid clicks, plays and cost per play are distinct metrics. Its creative format is 16:9. Compare matching-date ad results and downstream engagement, rather than importing Home qPTR or YouTube percentage claims. [Ads Manager](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ads-manager)

No reviewed primary source establishes a reproducible 5–8% CTR recipe for this game. Unverified CTR predictor scores, anecdotal percentages without creatives/cohorts, and YouTube-specific expression statistics are not used as Roblox performance evidence.

## Actual game grounding

Read-only checks identified the correct Glass vs Glass Studio (universe 10770493435/place 129420351303019), currently in Edit. No scene or gameplay mutations were made, and no fresh Studio render is claimed.

- **Frost:** original Frostglobe asset; its PowerFX kit produces a frost cone and angular ice spikes at the target. The illustration avoids a giant ice prison or permanent immobilization.
- **Bomb:** ArenaRules attaches a timed bomb to the opponent; ArenaFX draws a dark smooth sphere with an orange fuse nub that blinks. The concept uses one attached hazard, without a fake digital countdown or instant-kill promise.
- **Heal race:** Patch is a green glass sphere/core with a heart marker and a limited HP heal, collectable by a fighter. The picture represents a contest for the pickup, not currency or a paid reward.
- **Bumpers:** the twist builds cyan glass spheres with white cores on thin rods. The picture shows a rebound, not a spiked trap or an instant-kill post.

These new round-layer concepts should accompany the matching published revamp. The session only creates marketing art; it does not publish game changes.

## How the new tests differ

01 emphasizes proximity; 02 emphasizes fracture visibility. They are focused generative edits, not pixel-controlled experiments; small incidental drift remains possible.

03 changes the opponent/emotional relationship. 04 changes to an identifiable frost-power attack. These are broader story tests.

05–07 were added in response to the creator's request for distinct new concepts: a hazard attached to the hero, two rivals racing for a resource, and a surprising arena collision. They keep large expressions, strong color separation and a small number of subjects while changing the event itself.

All seven are unmeasured. A creative qualifies as useful through actual acquisition and player engagement, not because a prediction score promises a high CTR.
