Cracked Close Call is the provisional leader at similar impression counts. It has twice Lid Block's clicks and 3.6 times Cast's raw clicks, with only a small difference in exposure. These are descriptive comparisons; audience allocation is not known to be randomized.

The useful creative hypothesis is an obvious vulnerable subject, a large readable expression and an approaching threat. Cast's simpler backdrop/glass treatment did not by itself produce a strong early result in this crop. Do not conclude that text always fails, all faces win, the blue background caused the difference, or the original Cast was outperformed in a matched test; those variables and audiences are not isolated.

Keep Cracked Close Call unchanged as the comparison image while collecting the next matching-date checkpoint. If its lead persists, prioritize variations of this same danger story and alter one major feature at a time. There is no basis here for increasing spend or promising a 4–5% CTR.

Only 18 clicks support the lead so far. Illustrative 95% Wilson ranges: Cast 0.35–1.93%; Block 0.73–2.62%; Close Call 1.78–4.37%. Ranges overlap; they are not proof of equal performance or a controlled experiment.

Attributed plays and subsequent engagement are outside the crop. No conclusion about qualified-player conversion or acquisition cost follows from CTR alone. No artwork or live campaign settings were changed for this update.
