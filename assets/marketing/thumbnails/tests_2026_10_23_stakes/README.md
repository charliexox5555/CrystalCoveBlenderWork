## What changed from the earlier round

The design hypothesis is that an obvious event and emotional reaction can make the glassies more inviting than a calm product portrait or distant collection. Last Heart adds a recognizable risk from the real three-heart system; Bottle Breaks Dragon shows an unexpected hit without asserting an automatic win, purchasable advantage or changed gameplay rules. Saturated power trails are tied to the collision, with no logo, headline or decorative crowd.

The screenshot currently favors Power Duel (2.4%, 327 impressions, about eight clicks) and Bear Panic (2.1%, 283 impressions, about six clicks). Small counts and unmatched delivery prevent a firm winner or a claim that any particular face, effect, logo or palette caused the difference. The old Unicorn's newer result is 1.4%/2,096, replacing its earlier provisional-leading snapshot.

For the acquisition objective test, reuse Power Duel and Bear Panic first in a separate Plays campaign, then add one of these new challengers at a time. Judge plays and cost/play alongside CTR. See [results, caveats and official objective guidance](RESULTS_AND_TEST_PLAN.md). No campaign, budget, Studio, gameplay or upload changes were made.
