## Interpretation

Power Duel (2.4%, about 8 clicks) and Bear Panic (2.1%, about 6 clicks) are the observed leaders, with small samples. The original Unicorn is now at 1.4% from 2,096 impressions, superseding its earlier 2.3%/663 snapshot. It is not the present CTR leader. Expressive action is worth pursuing, but different audience/time/delivery mixes and the small samples prevent causal claims about faces, effects, colors or logos.

The acquisition bottleneck cannot be isolated from this crop: high cost/play can involve auction cost, CTR, click-to-play conversion and objective choice. Plays and qualified plays are not the same as ad clicks. A 4–5% CTR is a goal, not an established requirement or guarantee of economical joins.

## Objective recommendation and test sequence

[Roblox Ads Manager](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ads-manager), checked 23 October 2026, says Plays targets users likely to start sessions, while Engagement targets a smaller age-checked highly engaged audience and typically has higher cost/play. Engagement is aimed at progress toward Kids/Select eligibility. Campaign goal is fixed after creation. Attributed plays/earnings can take up to 48 hours to appear.

For a primary goal of cheaper player acquisition, a separate Plays campaign is a sensible test; retain Engagement separately only if its eligibility goal is intentional. Do not promise a specific cost reduction. First reuse Power Duel and Bear Panic in the Plays test so the objective comparison is not completely confounded by brand-new artwork. Then introduce one challenger at a time from this round. Keep dates/audience settings comparable and review asset-level impressions, clicks, CTR, plays, click-to-play conversion and CPP; also check subsequent engagement. Do not blend Engagement CTR with Plays CTR or organic Home qPTR.

No campaign creation, budget change or upload was performed. The creator controls whether/how much to spend. All advice is a test plan using the current objective documentation and supplied data; there is no claimed universal Roblox CTR benchmark.
