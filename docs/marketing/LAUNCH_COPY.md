# Glass Clash: launch copy

Drafted 2026-10-21 for the group test (Thu Oct 22) and the public release (Sat Oct 24). Kid-friendly, no links in the
experience description (Roblox strips or flags them), no promises of free Robux.

## Experience description (Roblox, max 1,000 characters)

```
🔮 Collect glassies. Swing them. SHATTER your rival!

Step on a pad, pick a glassy from your hand, pull back, and let go! Two glass fighters swing on their strings and smash together until one shatters. Every glassy has its own power, so every round is a surprise.

✨ WHAT'S INSIDE
• 40+ glassies to collect, from soda bottles to glass dragons and unicorns
• Powers you can CAST at the perfect moment
• Crown Hill: win to become King and defend your crown
• Flyers to ride and finishers to show off your win
• Open the Kiln to find new glassies (odds always shown)
• Daily rewards with exclusive glassies on Day 2 and Day 7
• Level up and show off your profile

🎁 Join the group for a free Marble Kiln open!
🏷️ Use code RELEASE for 300 coins!

Made by Midnights Studio. Tap the feedback button in game to tell us what you think!
```
