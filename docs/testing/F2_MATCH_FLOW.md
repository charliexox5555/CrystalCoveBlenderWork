# F2 match flow

Seven tagged arenas now run independent queues and matches. Enter a queue pad to join, step off or press Leave to cancel, and a bot fills an empty side after 15 seconds. Leaving requires stepping off before automatic re-entry; Play Again deliberately requeues at the same arena. Arena labels appear only for queues/running matches and hide locally during the participant camera.

MatchRunner advances Roll → Aim → Countdown → Fight → RoundEnd → next round/results through MatchLogic. It preserves the one free reroll across rounds and refuses every paid reroll. Picks must come from offers, all gameplay remotes are limited, aim must be finite, and player ownership/phase are checked on the server. Collections and bot collections are filtered to installed Body + Shards assets; the place currently has the three starters.

FightService runs multiple pendulum sessions. FightView renders one scene per fight; only the participant gets input, HUD and camera. Nearby spectators receive spatial effects/snapshots; remote fights outside 120 studs are culled. Other arenas' audio is muted while the participant watches their fight. Shards remain client-only and use the shared mobile cap.

Rewards use MatchLogic and are session-only until DataService is added. Disconnect/reset forfeits through MatchLogic; duplicate round-end calls cannot remove extra hearts or grant twice.

## Studio helpers

```lua
local dev = game.ServerStorage.MatchDev
dev:Invoke("Start", 1, game.Players:GetPlayers()[1], nil) -- side A vs bot
dev:Invoke("Start", 2, nil, game.Players:GetPlayers()[1]) -- side B vs bot
dev:Invoke("AllBots") -- all idle arenas; Arena 1 gets the QA camera
dev:Invoke("Inspect")
-- Client: game.Players.LocalPlayer.PlayerScripts.FightClientDev:Invoke("Inspect")
-- Client: ...:Invoke("DropEvery", 3) drops every third unreliable snapshot for QA.
```

All helpers are Studio-only. Runtime labels are generated by ArenaService; no builder was run over manual fences/decor.
