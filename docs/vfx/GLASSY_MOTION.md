# Living glassies (GlassyMotion)

Workstream E, Oct 19 2026, for the creator's Priority 4 ask: animated components, moving parts, idle motion and distinct reactions.

## Data

- **Source of truth:** the Blender motion manifests, `docs/art/glassy_revamp/motion_manifest.json` (24 release glassies, v3) and `docs/art/launch_glassies/motion_manifest.json` (12 launch glassies, v4). Each part has a pivot (model space, studs at GlassyScale 1, origin at the hang point), an axis, an idle (plus optional `idle_layers`), a hit reaction, `lod_keep` and `follows`.
- **Config:** `src/shared/Config/GlassyMotion.luau`, generated by `lune run tools/gen_glassy_motion` and then `stylua`. The converter is pure (`src/shared/GlassyMotionConvert.luau`) and covered by `src/tests/GlassyMotionTests.luau`.
- **What the config keeps:** only parts that move, or that ride a moving part. Parents come before followers. Static parts and `none` hits are dropped.
- **Current numbers:** 36 glassies, 207 moving parts.

## Runtime (`src/client/Presentation/GlassyMotion.luau`)

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Idle | sway, spin, bob, pulse, flicker, blink, drip, and slosh (the liquid lags the swing, using the body's angular velocity about the part's axis with a damped spring). A follower applies its parent's motion first: the fish tail rides the fish, the eyes blink on it. |
| Hits | kick (signed by hit side), flare, hop, squash, flash (Neon for its duration) and spin_boost, rung by a damped spring. Strength comes from workstream B's tier (`Feel:Classify`): Glance 0.4, Solid 0.7, Smash 1, Crit 1.3, Block 0.5. In fights everything is ×0.6, so detail reactions stay under B's whole-body wobble; the glassy that took no damage reacts at 0.6. |
| Ready | While the power is Ready (and while armed), idle runs ×1.7 speed and ×1.35 amplitude. |
| Cast | Every reacting part fires its reaction: 1.0 for a manual cast, 0.6 for an auto cast. |
| Body idle | Per type, through `Hero.motion` (rotation about the hang point): Heavy sways slowly ≤1.4°, Fizzy bobs ≤0.03 studs, Sharp shimmers quickly ≤0.6°. It fades out for 0.4 s after any hit, so it never fights B's wobble. |
| Reduced motion | `UI.Components.Motion.reduced()` (cached every second): half amplitude and no body idle. |
| Cost | One `BulkMoveTo` per glassy per frame. Fights you aren't watching (`f.LowDetail`) rest their parts once and skip motion. Previews share one 30 Hz loop that skips hidden portraits and drops destroyed ones. |

## Where it runs

- **Fights:** `Hero.init` attaches it; `SignaturePresentation.status` steps it on the fight clock (hitstop and slow-mo apply) and skips the name-based v2 detail block for that fighter. Hits, Ready and cast arrive through PowerFX (`Hero.state`). Prism's lens is skipped while it aims.
- **UI portraits** (`UI/Components/GlassyPortrait`): inventory, collection, shop/Kiln item cards, the match hand cards and Pick Any all use this component.
- **Lobby showcase and bundle stage** demo copies.

## Gate (no effect on today's models)

A model animates only if its source `ReplicatedStorage.Assets.Glassies.<id>` meets both conditions:
1. It is flagged as a v3/v4 import: attribute `MotionVersion >= 3` (or `ReleaseVersion >= 3`, or it contains `Pivot_<Detail>` markers).
2. It has a config entry.

When the importer brings `Pivot_<Detail>` in as Attachments or parts, those positions win over `pivot_roblox`. The installed v2 models are not flagged, so nothing they show changes until the v4 bundles are installed with `MotionVersion = 4` (requested from A on Oct 19).

## Split with workstream B (agreed Oct 19)

B keeps whole-body squash, wobble, jolt and recoil (`f.React`) and every camera and screen beat. E owns the Details parts and the tiny body idle. `Scene:Pose` composes hang × tilt/twist × `Hero.motion` (which includes the body idle) × `f.React`, so B's wobble rides on top.
